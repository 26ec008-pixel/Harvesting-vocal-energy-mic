# Harvesting Vocal Energy — Self-Powered Acoustic Microphone

## Overview
**Harvesting Vocal Energy** is a sustainable hardware innovation project designed to capture ambient and direct vocal sound waves and convert them into electrical energy. The system integrates an **Audio Capsule Core** for clear voice pickup alongside a **Vocal Energy Harvesting Ring** that harvests acoustic vibrations to trickle-charge an internal power cell.

## Problem Statement
Wireless microphones and portable audio devices rely heavily on disposable or externally charged batteries, leading to frequent power interruptions during live events and increased electronic waste.

## Proposed Solution
By embedding piezoelectric or electromagnetic acoustic transducers around the microphone head (the **Vocal Energy Harvesting Ring**), the mechanical vibrations generated by human speech are converted into electrical energy while maintaining high-fidelity audio transmission through the central **Audio Capsule Core**.

## Key Components (TRL-1 Concept)
* **Audio Capsule Core:** Captures clean vocal audio signals for standard sound output.
* **Vocal Energy Harvesting Ring:** Surrounds the core with acoustic transducers to capture sound pressure waves and convert them into micro-currents.
* **Power Management & Charge Indicator:** Regulates harvested energy to charge an internal battery, indicated by an active charging display on the handle.
